hi im jason. this is my website. it's dedicated to the kalabaw (carabao). they are a domestic water buffalo native to the philippines. they are my favorite animal and im super excited to help you learn more about them yayaayayyayayayayayayay.

a little bit about why I like them: there's a lot of them in where my family is from in the philippines. seeing them resting so cutely in the mud or helping farmers in the rice paddies always caught my interest and such. they are extremely gently giants and you should look up videos of them swimming underwater. they're so cute.

the carabao is also the national animal of the philippines. it represents hard work, strength, and the everyday spirit of the filipino. they're also just all cute just like us fr.

some of their daily behaviors and hobbies include:

  • chilling in mud to cool off since they dont have sweat glands.
  • eating grass and aquatic weeds
  • helping farmers plow fields due to their brute strength
